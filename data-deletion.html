Request data deletion

If you are a guest and your request concerns a stay or enquiry, contact the accommodation provider first so it can identify the relevant booking and determine which records it controls.

For a request concerning data processed directly by the RECEPTORA project, email receptoracz@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. Include enough information to locate the relevant conversation, but do not send passwords, payment card details or identity documents by email.

We may need to verify your request and coordinate with the accommodation provider. Some information may need to be retained where required by law. We will explain the outcome of your request.

Before publication, configure and document the real deletion workflow, response timeline, backup handling and lawful retention exceptions.
