PRIVACY

Request data deletion

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data processed in connection with RECEPTORA AI.

1. Send your request

Email receptoracz@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request. Describe which WhatsApp conversation or service your request concerns, and provide only the minimum information needed to locate it. Do not email passwords, payment-card details or identity documents unless specifically and securely requested.

2. If your request concerns an accommodation booking

The accommodation provider may be the organisation responsible for booking and guest records. You can contact that provider directly, or contact RECEPTORA AI so we can help identify the appropriate organisation.

3. Verification and response

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the request concerns your data. We aim to respond without undue delay and, where the GDPR applies, generally within one month of receiving a valid request. If an extension is legally permitted and necessary, we will inform you and explain why.

4. What can be deleted

The current RECEPTORA AI backend has no separate database of WhatsApp message histories. We will check for personal data within systems we control and erase it where required and technically possible. Data held by the accommodation provider, Meta, Render or other independent providers may require separate handling. Some records may be retained where legally required or otherwise permitted; we will explain any relevant limitation.

5. Confirmation

We will reply to your email to explain the action taken, whether any data was found, and whether further steps with another responsible organisation are necessary.
