Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

RECEPTORA AI is a messaging integration project operated by Bui Quang Tung in the Czech Republic. For privacy enquiries, contact receptoracz@gmail.com.

1. Scope and responsibility

This notice describes the current RECEPTORA AI WhatsApp webhook integration. When a guest contacts an accommodation business, that business may be responsible for determining why and how guest information is used. RECEPTORA AI may process information on its behalf. The exact roles depend on the relationship and applicable law.

2. Information processed

WhatsApp webhook events may include sender identifiers and phone numbers, message text, attachments or related metadata, timestamps, delivery statuses and technical event details. The current application reads incoming webhook events to verify their authenticity and count received messages.

3. Current functionality and storage

The current RECEPTORA AI application does not implement a separate message database, message-history storage, automated AI responses or outgoing WhatsApp replies. Its application-level log statement records that a verified event was received and the number of messages; it is not designed to log guest phone numbers, message bodies or full webhook payloads.

Technical infrastructure may nevertheless process and retain operational logs, network metadata or other information according to its configuration and service policies. The absence of an application database does not mean that no personal data is processed or retained anywhere.

4. Purposes and lawful basis

Information is processed to receive and validate WhatsApp events, operate and secure the integration, diagnose technical failures and respond to privacy enquiries. Depending on the circumstances and the responsible organisation, processing may rely on legitimate interests in operating a secure service, steps relating to a requested service, contractual necessity or applicable legal obligations. The responsible controller must determine and document the appropriate basis for each use.

5. Service providers and international transfers

The current service uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform for messaging, Render for hosting the backend, and GitHub Pages for this public website. These providers may process information in different countries under their own service arrangements. Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, applicable transfer safeguards must be assessed and used.

6. Retention

The current backend does not intentionally store message histories in an application database. Operational logs and information held by service providers may be retained under their respective settings and policies. RECEPTORA AI reviews available records when responding to an individual request and does not promise immediate deletion of information controlled independently by a third party.

7. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights under the GDPR to access, correct or erase your personal data, restrict or object to processing, and request data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority, including the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ).

For information about an accommodation booking, you may contact the accommodation provider directly. For data handled by RECEPTORA AI, email receptoracz@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Security and changes

The backend checks the authenticity of WhatsApp webhook requests using Meta's signature mechanism. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. This notice will be updated when the system adds AI processing, persistent message storage, additional providers or other material functionality.
